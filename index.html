Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

Your privacy is paramount to us. Because PantryTalk operates using an entirely offline local database engine (Drift/SQLite), we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal grocery data, inventory snapshots, or voice transcripts to our own servers.

1. Data Storage

All item lists, kitchen inventories, dynamic profiles, and historical data remain encrypted locally within your device sandbox environment.

2. Third-Party Services

If you utilize optional voice parsing or cloud translations, raw string metadata may be processed statelessly through secure edge runtime wrappers to query AI validation layers (Gemini Flash). This data is completely ephemeral and never persisted permanently on remote logs.
