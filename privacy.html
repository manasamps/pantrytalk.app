PantryTalk

A premium, Modern Farmhouse lifestyle utility application.

PantryTalk is built on a local-first architecture. Your grocery lists, pantry inventory, expiry dates, and shopping history are stored on your device using an encrypted local database (Drift/SQLite). You can use the app fully offline as a guest without linking a cloud account.

In short: We do not operate a central server that stores your pantry or grocery lists. Optional cloud features (voice dictation and receipt scanning) send only the data needed for that request to our secure API, which forwards it to an AI provider for parsing. Receipt photos and voice audio are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers after the request completes.

3. Local storage on your device

When you use PantryTalk — including in Guest mode — the following stays on your phone or tablet inside the app’s secure sandbox:

  • Grocery list and pantry inventory
  • Item quantities, units, categories, and expiry information
  • Your chosen display name and kitchen language
  • Notification preferences and locally scheduled expiry reminders
  • A diagnostic log file used for troubleshooting (not uploaded automatically)

We do not upload your full inventory or grocery lists to PantryTalk servers. Uninstalling the app or using “delete local data” (when available in Settings) removes this information from your device.

4. Optional cloud features

Cloud processing is optional and requires you to sign in with Google and enable AI features in Settings. These features include:

Voice dictation

When you tap the microphone, audio is recorded on your device and sent over HTTPS to our API at api.pantrytalk.app. The API forwards it to Google’s Gemini model to extract item names and quantities. The audio is processed for that request only and is not retained on our infrastructure.

Receipt scanning

When you photograph, import, or share a receipt, the image or PDF is sent to the same API for parsing. Our system is instructed to extract product lines and ignore headers, totals, payment details, and card numbers where possible. The receipt file is not saved on our servers after parsing. Parsed items are shown to you for review before anything is saved — and only approved lines are written to your local pantry database.

Please note: Receipts may contain personal information (store name, partial payment details, loyalty IDs). Share only receipts you are comfortable processing, and review parsed lines before confirming.

Usage limits

To operate free-tier AI quotas, we store a pseudonymous device identifier and your signed-in user ID together with weekly parse counts. This does not include the content of your lists or receipts.

Ingredient matching contributions

If you explicitly enable “Help improve ingredient matching” in Settings, anonymised mapping votes may be sent to improve shared dictionary quality. This is off by default and can be disabled at any time.

5. Third-party processors

We use trusted providers to deliver authentication and AI parsing:

  • Supabase — Google Sign-In authentication (email, user ID)
  • Google — Sign-In OAuth; Gemini AI for voice and receipt parsing
  • Cloudflare — API hosting, edge security, and quota storage (KV)

Each provider processes data only as needed to perform their service. Their own privacy policies apply to infrastructure they control. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide AI parsing when you request it, manage your account sign-in, and enforce fair-use quotas.
  • Consent — for optional ingredient-mapping contributions and, where required, before first use of cloud AI features. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep our API secure, prevent abuse, and improve reliability (balanced against your privacy rights).

Local-only use as a guest does not require cloud processing and is not dependent on consent for third-party AI.

7. Retention

  • On-device data: kept until you delete items, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • Voice audio & receipt files: not retained after the parse request completes.
  • Quota / usage counters: retained per weekly billing period, then reset or aggregated.
  • Authentication records: retained while your account is active; deleted on account deletion request.
  • Optional mapping votes: retained only as long as needed for the dictionary feature.

9. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — obtain confirmation and a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (most pantry data can be edited directly in the app).
  • Erasure — request deletion of cloud account data; delete local data via the app or uninstall.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where applicable.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — disable AI features, sign out, or turn off optional contributions.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority (e.g. your EU member-state supervisory authority).

To exercise these rights, email pantrytalk.app@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

10. Children

PantryTalk is not directed at children under 16 in the EEA/UK (or under 13 elsewhere) without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

We use HTTPS for all API communication, authenticated access for cloud features, and on-device sandboxing for local data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please keep your device and Google account credentials protected.
