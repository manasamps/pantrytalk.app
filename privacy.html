PantryTalk

A premium, Modern Farmhouse lifestyle utility application.

PantryTalk is built on a local-first architecture. Your pantry inventory, expiry dates, and shopping history are stored on your device using an encrypted local database (Drift/SQLite). Grocery lists also live on your device by default. You can use the app fully offline as a guest without linking a cloud account.

In short: Pantry and grocery data stay on your phone unless you choose optional cloud features. If you share a grocery list with someone, that list’s items are stored in our cloud database so invited members can edit in real time — sharing is invite-only (not public or searchable). We do not use that data for advertising. Optional cloud AI (voice dictation and receipt scanning) sends only what is needed for each request; audio and receipt files are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers after the request completes.

3. Local storage on your device

When you use PantryTalk — including in Guest mode — the following stays on your phone or tablet inside the app’s secure sandbox:

  • Grocery lists (unless you explicitly share a list to the cloud — see §5)
  • Pantry inventory (always local — not uploaded for sync or sharing)
  • Item quantities, units, categories, and expiry information
  • Your chosen display name and kitchen language
  • Notification preferences, staple reminder selections, and locally scheduled expiry reminders
  • A diagnostic log file used for troubleshooting (not uploaded automatically)

We do not upload your pantry inventory to PantryTalk servers. Private grocery lists remain on your device only. Uninstalling the app or using “delete local data” (when available in Settings) removes this information from your device.

4. Optional cloud features

Cloud processing is optional and requires you to sign in with Google and enable AI features in Settings. These features include:

Voice dictation

When you tap the microphone, audio is recorded on your device and sent over HTTPS to our API at api.pantrytalk.app. The API forwards it to Google’s Gemini model to extract item names and quantities. The audio is processed for that request only and is not retained on our infrastructure.

Receipt scanning

When you photograph, import, or share a receipt, the image or PDF is sent to the same API for parsing. Our system is instructed to extract product lines and ignore headers, totals, payment details, and card numbers where possible. The receipt file is not saved on our servers after parsing. Parsed items are shown to you for review before anything is saved — and only approved lines are written to your local pantry database.

Please note: Receipts may contain personal information (store name, partial payment details, loyalty IDs). Share only receipts you are comfortable processing, and review parsed lines before confirming.

Usage limits

Cloud AI features (voice dictation and receipt scanning) are subject to fair-use usage limits (for example, a weekly parse quota). These limits are not a paid subscription or billing cycle — PantryTalk does not charge for AI parses. To enforce fair use, we store a pseudonymous device identifier and your signed-in user ID together with parse counts. This does not include the content of your lists or receipts.

Limits may be adjusted at any time at the developer’s discretion (for example, to manage cost, abuse, or service quality). We will not use your grocery or pantry content to set personalised limits or for advertising.

Ingredient matching contributions

If you explicitly enable “Help improve ingredient matching” in Settings, anonymised mapping votes may be sent to improve shared dictionary quality. This is off by default and can be disabled at any time.

5. Shared grocery lists (your choice)

Live grocery list sharing is entirely optional. Nothing is uploaded until you tap Share with someone on a list (or join someone else’s list with an invite code). Each shared list supports two people, and you may participate in up to five active shared lists at a time. You must be signed in with Google to use this feature. PantryTalk is not a public social network — shared lists are invite-only, not discoverable in the app, and visible only to people who receive a valid invite code or link from you or the list owner.

When you choose to share or join:

  • The shared list’s name, items (names, quantities, units, categories, bought/checked state), and membership (who is owner or member) are stored in Supabase Postgres so participants can sync edits in real time.
  • Your pantry inventory stays on your device and is not shared through this feature. Only the list owner can move bought items from a shared list into their local pantry.
  • Invite codes and https://pantrytalk.de/join.html?code=… links let others join; the list owner can stop sharing, which archives the cloud list and invalidates the invite.
  • You may also export a plain-text snapshot of a list through your device’s share sheet (for example, to Messages or WhatsApp). That export is initiated by you and leaves PantryTalk through the operating system — we do not control how recipients use it.
  • If you never share and never join, your grocery lists behave as before — local only.

Shared list data is used solely to provide the sharing feature (sync, invites, membership). It is not used for advertising, sold to data brokers, or used to build advertising profiles. We do not run automated text moderation on list names or item names; see §6 for how to report abuse.

6. Reporting concerns (shared lists & abuse)

Shared lists are meant for household or trusted contacts. If another member posts offensive, harassing, or unlawful content in a shared list, or if you believe someone is misusing the feature:

  • Leave the list — stop using it and, if you wish, delete your local copy from the app.
  • Ask the owner to stop sharing — this ends cloud sync and invalidates the invite code for new joins.
  • Report to us — email pantrytalk.app@gmail.com with a description of the concern, the list name if known, and your signed-in account email. We will review reasonable reports and may suspend cloud access or shared lists that violate our Terms of Service.

We do not provide in-app chat, public profiles, or content feeds. Reporting and owner controls are how we address misuse without automated filtering of grocery item text.

7. Advertising & data use

PantryTalk does not show third-party advertisements in the app and does not sell your personal data. Grocery lists you choose to store in the cloud for sharing, your pantry data, voice input, receipt images, and account information are not used for targeted advertising or shared with advertising networks.

We use third-party services only as described in this policy (authentication, AI parsing, hosting, quotas) to operate the app — not to monetise your content through ads.

8. Your responsibilities

When you enable cloud AI features, you are responsible for how you use them. Before the first voice or receipt parse, the app asks you to confirm that you understand and accept the following:

  • You review every AI result before saving items — nothing is added automatically without your confirmation in the review step.
  • AI may misread product names, quantities, prices, or categories — you decide what to keep in your pantry or grocery list.
  • You only submit receipts and voice input you have the right to process, and you accept that receipts may contain personal or payment-related information.
  • Expiry notifications are reminders only — not food-safety or medical advice.
  • If you share a grocery list, you choose who receives the invite code; shared list content is visible to everyone who joins that list. Do not use shared lists for harassment, hate speech, or unlawful content.
  • If you export a list as plain text through your device’s share sheet, you are responsible for where that text is sent.

If we materially change cloud AI responsibilities, we will update this policy and may ask you to accept the updated terms in the app before using those features again.

10. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide AI parsing when you request it, manage your account sign-in, enforce fair-use quotas, and sync shared grocery lists when you choose to share or join.
  • Consent — for optional ingredient-mapping contributions and, where required, before first use of cloud AI features. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep our API secure, prevent abuse, and improve reliability (balanced against your privacy rights).

Local-only use as a guest does not require cloud processing and is not dependent on consent for third-party AI.

11. Retention

  • On-device data: kept until you delete items, clear app data, or uninstall.
  • Shared grocery lists (cloud): kept while the list is active and you remain a member; archived when the owner stops sharing; deleted on account deletion request where feasible.
  • Voice audio & receipt files: not retained after the parse request completes.
  • Quota / usage counters: retained per weekly usage period (currently reset each Monday, UTC), then reset or aggregated. This is a fair-use limit while the app remains free — not a billing cycle.
  • Authentication records: retained while your account is active; deleted on account deletion request.
  • Optional mapping votes: retained only as long as needed for the dictionary feature.

13. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — obtain confirmation and a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (most pantry data can be edited directly in the app).
  • Erasure — request deletion of cloud account data; delete local data via the app or uninstall.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where applicable.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — disable AI features, sign out, or turn off optional contributions.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority (e.g. your EU member-state supervisory authority).

To exercise these rights, email pantrytalk.app@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.
