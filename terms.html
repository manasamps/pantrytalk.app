5. Shared grocery lists

Live list sharing is optional. You choose when to share a list or join someone else’s list with an invite code. Before the first share or join, the app shows a short cloud-sharing notice; by continuing you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy for list sharing. Each live shared list is limited to two people. You may share or join up to five active shared lists at a time. PantryTalk is a household utility — not a public social network. Shared lists are invite-only and not discoverable in the app. By sharing or joining, you agree that the shared list’s content (list name, items, and check-off state) will be stored in our cloud database and visible to other members of that list.

  • Only the list owner (the person who shared the list) can move bought items from a shared list into their local pantry.
  • The owner may stop sharing at any time; the invite code will stop working and members keep their last synced copy without further updates.
  • Do not share invite codes publicly if you intend the list to stay private to people you trust.
  • You may export a plain-text snapshot of a list through your device’s share sheet (for example, to WhatsApp). That is separate from live cloud sharing and is your responsibility.
  • Shared list data is used only to operate the sharing feature — not for advertising (see our Privacy Policy).

We do not guarantee uninterrupted sync or permanent cloud storage of shared lists. You should keep your own copy of important shopping information.
