Delete your PantryTalk account

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your PantryTalk cloud account and associated data. PantryTalk is developed by an individual developer. Contact: pantrytalk.app@gmail.com.

1. Delete cloud account data (signed-in users)

If you signed in with Google, follow these steps:

  1. Open the PantryTalk app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Sign out if you are currently signed in (optional but recommended before deletion).
  4. Email pantrytalk.app@gmail.com from the same Google address you used in the app with the subject line “PantryTalk account deletion request”.
  5. We will verify your identity and delete your cloud account data within 30 days (usually sooner).

What we delete (where applicable):

  • Google-linked authentication records (email, user ID)
  • Shared grocery lists you own (and membership for lists you joined)
  • Cloud usage / quota counters tied to your account
  • Optional ingredient-mapping votes linked to your account

What we may keep temporarily:

  • Backups or logs needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance (retained only as long as necessary, then deleted)
  • Shared list content created by another owner if you were only a member — your membership is removed; the owner’s copy may remain until they delete it

2. Delete local data on your device (all users)

You can remove on-device data without deleting a cloud account:

  • Delete individual grocery or pantry items in the app
  • Delete grocery lists from the list switcher menu
  • Leave a shared list (removes your membership; does not delete the owner’s list)
  • Uninstall the app or clear PantryTalk app data in Android Settings — this removes local grocery lists, pantry inventory, and guest profile data on that device

3. Guest users (no Google sign-in)

If you used Continue as Guest, there is no cloud account to delete. Uninstall the app or clear app data on your device to remove local information.

4. Retention summary

  • Voice audio & receipt images: not stored after parsing completes
  • On-device data: until you delete items, clear app data, or uninstall
  • Cloud account data: deleted after a verified deletion request, typically within 30 days
